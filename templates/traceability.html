What the download columns mean

Hugo — scores CSV

  • Company — the company name.
  • Assessed Score — its 0-10 commercial score for this run (signal_strength + recency + corroboration).
  • Coverage — how many of the 6 signal types had evidence (e.g. "3 of 6"). A high score with low coverage = one strong signal.
  • Outreach Eligible — YES if score ≥ 8 (worth contacting).
  • Signals Found — number of evidenced signals.

Maya — trajectory CSV

  • Company — the company name.
  • Current Score — its score in this run.
  • Trajectory — previous run → this run (e.g. "6.5→8.0"), or "X (new)" on first appearance.
  • Movement — RISING / FADING / stable / NEW.
  • Delta — the signed change (+1.5 = gained 1.5 points).
