Where this score comes from

Every score is deterministic: score = signal strength (0–6) + recency (0–2) + corroboration (0–2), computed per signal, then averaged over the signals actually found ( here) — never over all six categories.

Signal strength · 0–6

Commercial relevance of the event, judged by the interpretation model from the isolated evidence. Shown per signal below via its confidence label.

Recency · 0–2

≤90 days → 2 · ≤6 months → 1 · undated → 0. Open the source news below to check the date yourself.

Corroboration · 0–2

2+ independent URLs → 2 · one verified URL → 1 · no URL → 0. Re-derived per signal below from the stored sources.

Only proven, dated signals are scored; unproven categories are listed as not evidenced rather than penalised. Every claim below traces to a source URL.
